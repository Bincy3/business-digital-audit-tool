About Digital Audit Pro

A smarter way to capture website audit results with history tracking.

Built with Flask, Bootstrap 5, and SQLite, this audit tool captures every report, lets you revisit past audits, and stores HTML and TXT exports automatically.

  • Automatic audit history saved to SQLite.
  • Dashboard-style history with search, filter, sort, pagination, and score cards.
  • Download or delete saved reports with one click.

How it works

Each audit generates both an HTML and TXT report. The results are saved in the reports/ folder with a timestamped filename, and the audit metadata is stored in SQLite.

The Audit History page lets you quickly review past audits, sort by date or score, and manage report files from a professional SaaS-style dashboard.
